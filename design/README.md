# Eye Test — first visual concepts

Exploratory brand and product mockups generated with the built-in image generation tool. Players, footage, and counts are illustrative. These are raster concepts, not implemented screens or final vector assets. The logo board establishes the intended silhouette; the UI concepts vary in their rendering of the mark and need harmonizing during implementation.

- `logo-system.png`: primary lockup, reverse, and app icon.
- `player-library-dark.png`: dark film library.
- `clip-review-light.png`: ivory clip review workspace.

Direction: no-look-pass silhouette, athletic typography, warm ivory, charcoal, and restrained burnt orange. Put watching player footage first.

## Generation prompts

### 1. Logo system

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished landscape brand identity concept board for "EYE TEST", a basketball film viewing and clipping app named for judging players by watching them. Main hero: an original crisp flat vector-like white silhouette of a basketball player making a no-look pass inside a compact tall rounded rectangular black badge. Crucial pose: head in clear profile looking LEFT while one arm extends RIGHT passing a basketball at waist height, bent athletic legs, two arms only, two legs only, anatomically coherent, ball separate and obvious, no facial detail except profile. Classic sports silhouette simplicity, but original silhouette and proportions, no NBA letters or red-blue split. Pair badge with large confident condensed uppercase "EYE TEST" wordmark. Warm ivory background, black ink, restrained burnt orange accent. Art directed grid: large primary lockup upper left; large standalone silhouette upper right; lower strip with small black-on-ivory and white-on-black logo variants and compact app icon. Minimal tasteful typography labels "EYE TEST", "PRIMARY MARK", "REVERSE", "APP ICON". Very clean agency presentation, generous whitespace, flat artwork, no mock physical products, no gradients or shadows. Legible original no-look pass is highest priority.

### 2. Dark player library

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity desktop app screen concept for "EYE TEST", a basketball film library and automatic clip creation project. Landscape 16:10, straight-on full interface, exceptionally polished sports editorial product design. Charcoal-black background with warm ivory typography, muted gray rules, restrained burnt-orange selected states. Left narrow sidebar with white original no-look passing basketball-player silhouette badge and condensed EYE TEST wordmark, navigation "Players", "Splice", "Upload / Download", "Processes"; Players selected. Main area heading "Trust your eyes." with small eyebrow "THE FILM ROOM", search field "Find a player", simple team and season filters. Under heading a beautifully composed grid of six player film collection cards, cinematic cropped basketball action photographs on courts, authentic athletic movement, neutral unbranded uniforms. Names "Jayson Tatum", "Shai Gilgeous-Alexander", "Nikola Jokić", "Anthony Edwards", "Caitlin Clark", "Victor Wembanyama"; each card shows small clip count and "View film" affordance. Tiny discreet footer "Concept • Sample library". Strong typographic hierarchy, generous horizontal spacing, magazine-style large photos, square-ish modest radius cards, no stats dashboard graphs, no excessive pills, no neon glow. Treat names/counts as illustrative mock data. App chrome only, no monitor frame. Logo profile looks away from direction of pass.

### 3. Light clip review

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity desktop basketball clip-review application screen named "EYE TEST". Landscape 16:10, flat straight-on interface, sophisticated warm ivory and ink-black sports editorial style with burnt orange active controls, crisp grid, condensed large headings and readable sans UI. Left slim sidebar original black no-look-pass player silhouette badge with EYE TEST wordmark and nav "Players", "Splice", "Upload / Download", "Processes"; Splice selected. Main header "Find the play. Keep the context." small eyebrow "SPLICE / REVIEW". Four-step top progress "1 Game data", "2 Match plays", "3 Review", "4 Generate" with Review active. Main content a large basketball game video still taking two thirds of width showing a beautiful no-look pass on a hardwood court, neutral unbranded uniforms, realistic wide broadcast framing. Below video playback controls and a very clear clip range timeline with orange handles; labels "Start 01:24" and "End 01:36". Right column heading "Play details", selected player "Nikola Jokić", play type "Assist", quarter "Q2", game clock "08:42", action button "Save changes". Bottom a horizontal row of four modest basketball clip thumbnails with current one outlined orange, and right-aligned black "Generate clips" button. Small discreet "Concept • Sample footage" label. Excellent whitespace, restrained thin rules, no decorative charts, no giant rounded dashboard cards, no device frame. This is a design concept rather than actual app screenshot.
